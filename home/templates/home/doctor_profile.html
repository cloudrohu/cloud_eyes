Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Patients Often Ask

Find answers to some common questions about consultations, treatments and eye care.

Regular eye examinations can help identify changes in vision and detect certain eye conditions at an early stage. Your eye specialist can recommend an examination schedule based on your individual needs.
Cataract surgery is a commonly performed procedure. Your ophthalmologist will assess your eye health, discuss suitable treatment options and explain the procedure and recovery process before surgery.
A consultation may include a discussion about your symptoms and medical history, followed by appropriate eye examinations and diagnostic tests depending on your condition.
Suitability for LASIK depends on several factors, including eye health, prescription stability and corneal condition. A detailed examination is required to determine whether the procedure is appropriate.
The consultation time can vary depending on the patient's symptoms, examination requirements and diagnostic tests.
Follow-up requirements depend on the treatment or procedure performed. Your doctor will provide personalized guidance regarding follow-up visits and care.
