Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Find answers to common questions about eye care, treatments, consultations and procedures at Cloud Eyes.

Regular eye examinations can help detect changes in vision and identify certain eye conditions at an early stage. Your eye specialist can suggest an appropriate examination schedule based on your individual needs.

A cataract occurs when the natural lens of the eye becomes cloudy, which can gradually affect clear vision and everyday activities.

Cataract surgery is a commonly performed procedure. Your ophthalmologist will evaluate your eye health and discuss the appropriate treatment and recovery process with you.

LASIK suitability depends on factors such as eye health, prescription stability and corneal condition. A detailed eye examination is required before deciding whether LASIK is appropriate.

The consultation may include a discussion of your symptoms and medical history, followed by appropriate eye examinations and diagnostic tests based on your needs.

Follow-up requirements depend on the treatment or procedure. Your doctor will provide personalized instructions regarding follow-up visits and care.

Consultation time can vary depending on your symptoms, examination requirements and any diagnostic tests that may be needed.

If you experience changes in vision, persistent eye discomfort, blurred vision or other concerns, consulting an eye specialist can help determine the appropriate next step.
